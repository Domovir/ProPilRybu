# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the ProPilRybu project a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to creating an open, friendly and professional environment focused on collaboration, learning and constructive discussion.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate.
* Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
* Helping others learn and improve.
* Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
* Focusing discussions on technical topics and project improvement.
* Acting professionally in all project interactions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
* Personal attacks, insults or offensive language.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions or project activities.
* Any behavior that creates an intimidating or hostile environment.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull Requests
* Discussions
* Project documentation
* Any other official communication channels related to the project

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a private communication with the project maintainer through GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and handled confidentially whenever possible.

---

## Project Philosophy

The ProPilRybu project values:

* Reliability over complexity.
* Documentation over assumptions.
* Stability over unnecessary changes.
* Collaboration over competition.
* Continuous improvement through careful engineering.
